What it is about.   

A phone assistant that takes calls, books appointments and notes call-backs. An inbox that sorts itself and prepares replies. A chat on your website that knows your prices and opening hours. Set up for your business, with your rules.

The phone rings while you are with a customer. The inbox holds the same five questions as last week. And in the evening someone writes on the website asking whether you are open on Saturday. Each enquiry is quick to answer, together they eat your day.

AI assistants can help with recurring requests across phone, inbox or website chat. We scope the appropriate channels, approved information sources and integrations, then test the responses and human-review steps with your team.

Together, we define the approved business information and what the assistant may do. We test for uncertainty, sensitive requests and incorrect answers, and agree when it should stop and refer the conversation to a person.

We start by reviewing the task, information sources and integrations. A focused test helps establish what the assistant can handle reliably and where a person should take over.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask our team directly.

01Does the caller know they are talking to an AI?

The assistant should identify itself as AI. We plan a clear handover or callback route for requests that need a person, subject to the channels and availability agreed for your business.

02What happens to the data?

Before connecting any data, we review what is needed, provider terms, access controls, retention and hosting locations. The setup and any required privacy notices or agreements must be confirmed for your use case before deployment.

03Does it replace my staff?

No. It takes the recurring questions off their hands so there is time for the conversations that need a person. With anything tricky, the assistant hands over.

04How is the proposal prepared?

We discuss what the assistant needs to do, usage levels and integrations first. Setup and ongoing support are scoped around those requirements before we quote.

05When does it start?

We begin after a requirements conversation and an agreed scope. The suggested 2–6 week estimate depends on the integrations, knowledge sources, testing and approvals your project needs.
