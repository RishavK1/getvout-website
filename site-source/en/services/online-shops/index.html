What it is about.   

A shop that fits your products, not the other way round. Products, variants, payment, shipping and invoices work together, and running it stays simple. If you like, connected to your stock, a print-on-demand service or your accounting.

A good online shop feels like your brand. We design around your products, how customers compare them and what they need to know before buying, then build the catalogue and checkout around the agreed requirements.

We connect the agreed parts of your shop: catalogue, variants, checkout, shipping, order notifications and customer accounts. Payments and any inventory or accounting integrations are selected for your market, provider eligibility and business requirements.

Running it stays simple: add products, process orders, done. You get a training session and a direct contact when something new should be added later.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask our team directly.

01How do you scope an online shop?

We start by discussing your requirements. Once the scope, integrations and priorities are clear, we agree a tailored quote before the work begins.

02Which payment methods are possible?

We review suitable providers for your business and market, including cards, UPI or other supported methods where available. Provider approval, fees and supported currencies must be confirmed before integration.

03Can I manage products and orders myself?

We set up the agreed catalogue and order-management tools, then show your team how to use them. Roles, permissions and the handover are discussed as part of the project scope.

04Are the legal texts included?

We can build the pages and acceptance flows for policies you supply or approve with your legal adviser. Legal drafting and compliance advice are not assumed to be included in development.
