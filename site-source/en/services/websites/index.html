What it is about.   

No theme, no template, no graveyard of plugins. Design and development by our team, built on Next.js and React. Fast to load, with a content management system where you edit your content yourself.

A website is often the first impression someone gets of your business. We start with your goals: what you do, who your customers are and what they should do after their visit. Together, we shape the structure, content and design before development begins.

We build responsive websites with a technical foundation suited to your project. Performance, accessible interactions and clear content structure are considered alongside page titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemaps and readable URLs.

Where content editing is part of the scope, we set up a content system around the fields your team needs and provide a handover. We are based in Karnal, India, and collaborate online, with in-person meetings arranged where practical.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask our team directly.

01How do you scope a website project?

We start by discussing your requirements. Once the scope, integrations and priorities are clear, we agree a tailored quote before the work begins.

02Can I edit the content myself?

Yes, when content management is included in the agreed scope. We define the fields you need and provide a handover so your team can make everyday updates.

03Will people find me on Google?

We plan the technical SEO foundations: performance, readable URLs, page titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap. Local search and content needs can be discussed too. We do not promise a particular search ranking.

04Do you come by for the first meeting?

We usually begin with an online conversation. In-person meetings in or around Karnal can be arranged when practical. We discuss the project and the next steps without an obligation to proceed.

05I already have a website. Is a rebuild worth it?

We review your current website before recommending a revision or rebuild. If a rebuild is appropriate, we plan content migration and redirects where needed, while explaining any SEO risks rather than promising no change.
