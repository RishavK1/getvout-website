1. About this notice

This notice explains how the current getvout website preview handles information. getvout is a digital studio team based in Karnal, Haryana, India. For privacy questions, write to contact@getvout.com.

2. Visiting the website

Delivering a page requires technical connection information, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information and time of the request. The local preview server can display access requests in its console. Production hosting, log retention and service providers must be confirmed before this notice is used on a public deployment.

3. Cookies and local storage

The interface uses browser storage for the theme and interactive discoveries. Sound starts enabled on each new visit and can be turned off for that visit. The website is English-only and has no language-preference control. These features are not advertising profiles. You can clear site storage in your browser to reset saved preferences.

4. Analytics

Analytics are disabled in this local preview. If analytics are introduced for a public release, the tools, information collected and any applicable consent requirements must be described here before they are enabled.

5. Enquiries

The contact and project-brief forms in this preview validate input in the browser but do not send or store a submitted message. The interface displays a notice explaining this. Avoid entering sensitive information into a preview.

If you choose to contact us by email or WhatsApp, the communication is handled through the provider you use. We may use information you send to respond, discuss a project and manage that conversation. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. External links and media

Project and social links lead to external websites. Email links can open another application. WhatsApp is a third-party service. These services can receive connection information and apply their own privacy notices. Project preview videos are served locally and do not load a third-party video player.

7. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit information, clear browser storage and avoid opening external services. For questions about information you have shared directly with getvout, including requests to correct or delete it where applicable, contact contact@getvout.com. Applicable legal obligations may affect how a request can be handled.

8. Before public release

This is a preview-stage notice, not a statement that an unverified production setup complies with every applicable law. The responsible legal entity, hosting arrangements, form delivery, retention periods and applicable privacy obligations need review before publication. This notice will be updated when those details are confirmed.
