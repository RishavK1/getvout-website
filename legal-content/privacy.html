1. About this notice

This notice explains how the getvout website handles information. getvout is a digital studio team based in Karnal, Haryana, India. For privacy questions, write to contact@getvout.com.

2. Visiting the website

Delivering a page requires technical connection information, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information and time of the request. The website is hosted by Vercel, which processes this information to serve the site, keep it secure and measure aggregate usage. Our team can see aggregate analytics and any technical logs the hosting provider makes available to us.

3. Cookies and local storage

The interface uses browser storage for the theme and interactive discoveries. Sound starts enabled on each new visit and can be turned off for that visit. The website is English-only and has no language-preference control. These features are not advertising profiles. You can clear site storage in your browser to reset saved preferences.

4. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to measure page views and understand how visitors reach the site. It is cookie-free and does not create advertising profiles or track visitors across other websites. It records the page visited, the referring site, device and browser category, and an approximate country derived from the request. Vercel processes this information on our behalf.

5. Enquiries

When you submit the contact form or the project brief, the details you enter are sent to our team by email so we can reply and discuss your project. This can include your name, email address, optional WhatsApp number, company, existing website, project selections, timing, requested add-ons and message. Delivery is handled by Resend, an email service provider acting on our behalf. We do not use these details for advertising and we do not sell them.

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and manage the project conversation, and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless we must keep records for legal or accounting reasons. You can ask us to correct or delete an enquiry by writing to contact@getvout.com.

If you choose to contact us by email or WhatsApp instead, the communication is handled through the provider you use. We may use information you send to respond, discuss a project and manage that conversation. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. External links and media

Project and social links lead to external websites. Email links can open another application. WhatsApp is a third-party service. These services can receive connection information and apply their own privacy notices. Project preview videos are served locally and do not load a third-party video player.

7. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit information, clear browser storage and avoid opening external services. For questions about information you have shared with getvout, including requests to access, correct or delete it where applicable, contact contact@getvout.com. Applicable legal obligations may affect how a request can be handled.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The version published here applies from the date it is posted. Registered business details for getvout are listed on the imprint page as they become available.
