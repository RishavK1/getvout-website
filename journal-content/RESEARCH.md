# Journal editorial and search notes

Prepared 23 November 2026 for the local preview.

## Scope

Seven independent explainers replace the reference site's stories. Broad subject
areas and the existing interactive presentation remain; the original anecdotes,
research narratives, personal claims, revenue headlines, trailer, copied prose
and displayed code snippets are not republished as getvout editorial work.
Demo mechanics remain reference-derived as requested; this is not a claim that
getvout independently authored or owns that implementation.

Articles are AI-assisted editorial drafts, visibly disclosed on each page.
They require human editorial approval before public publication. No expert review,
user study, conversion experiment or paid keyword-volume study is claimed.

## Search intent

`articles.json` records suggested topic phrases, not proven high-volume or
"best ranking" keywords. Search-result reconnaissance was used to locate primary
guidance, not to copy competitors. No access to Search Console, keyword planner,
rank tracking or audience analytics was provided.

| Article | Reader intent | Distinct contribution |

## Primary references consulted

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
  - No special requirements, AI files or schema for Google AI Overviews/AI Mode.
  - Indexing/snippet eligibility matters; inclusion is not guaranteed.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
  - Original value, reliable sourcing, accurate authorship and people-first purpose.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article
  - Organization authors are valid; accurate headline/image/date information.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video
  - Discoverability, stable media URLs and video-feature eligibility.
  - Click-to-load is retained for the user-requested interaction; this preview
    does not claim eligibility for Google video features.
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/error-prevention-legal-financial-data.html
  - Reversible, checked or review/confirm/correct alternatives for specified submissions.
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html
  - 24 × 24 CSS pixel level-AA minimum with exceptions; 44 × 44 enhanced.
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/
  - Plan alternatives based on what the audio/video communicates.
- https://web.dev/articles/inp
  - Good INP at or below 200 ms at field p75; not a universal animation duration.
- https://web.dev/articles/prefers-reduced-motion
  - Respect reduced-motion preferences without losing meaningful feedback.
- https://developer.chrome.com/blog/a-customizable-select
  - Chrome 135 implementation and progressive enhancement.
- https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Extensions/Forms/Customizable_select
  - Limited browser availability; check compatibility and fallback behavior.
