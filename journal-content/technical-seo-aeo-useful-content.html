Technical SEO helps search systems discover and interpret a page. AEO, often used to mean answer engine optimization, describes making information useful in answer-led experiences. Neither is a shortcut around original, accurate content that serves a real audience.

Does Google require special AEO markup?

No. Google’s guidance for AI Overviews and AI Mode says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations. A supporting page must be indexed and eligible to appear in Search with a snippet. Google also says no special AI files or schema are needed for these features.

This does not promise inclusion. A page can meet technical requirements and still not be crawled, indexed or selected. Treat “rank in AI answers” as a goal to investigate, not a result a development team can guarantee through a package of keywords.

What should a useful answer contain?

Begin with the reader’s actual question and a concise answer. Follow it with the conditions, an example and a practical next step. If a claim depends on a standard or a measurement, link the source and explain its scope. Avoid making every heading a variation of the same search phrase.

For a business website, service information should explain who it is for, what is included or discussed, and how to proceed. A location is useful when it genuinely helps the visitor; repeating “best agency in India” without evidence adds little understanding.

A technical SEO checklist for the build

  • Provide crawlable links to important service and article pages.
  • Give each page a descriptive title and a useful, distinct summary.
  • Keep essential explanations available as text, not only inside images or videos.
  • Use canonical URLs consistently and plan redirects when addresses change.
  • Make structured data agree with the visible article and its authorship.
  • Check mobile usability, broken destinations and interaction performance.

What structured data belongs on an article?

Google documents Article and BlogPosting markup for information such as the headline, representative image, author and dates. An organization can be the author. Do not manufacture an expert biography, an award or a date simply to populate a field. A clear link to the team behind the publication is more useful than a fictional credential.

Questions and direct answers can help readers even without FAQ markup. Adding schema does not guarantee a rich result or an AI citation. Start with the page people can read, then describe that page accurately in machine-readable form.

How should success be measured?

After a public launch, verify the site in Search Console and inspect indexing, relevant queries and useful visits. Connect those observations to business outcomes such as qualified enquiries.
